# Statement-by-statement coverage audit

Audit date: 2026-11-23. Sources: `paper/main.tex` and `paper/supplement.tex` in this repository. Line numbers refer to these source copies, not to the rendered PDF. This is a coverage audit, not a replacement for the build and axiom audit.

**Current registration scope (updated 2026-11-24):** the three primary configurations
select 20 theorems concerning growth/upper bounds, the main finite extrema, and
the source model. Additional supplementary finite examples and their dependent
thresholds are outside the current delivery scope. Their compiled development
is retained below as an optional extension. A `Checked` label records Lean
compilation of a mathematical conclusion; it does not imply that the optional
fourth configuration completed independent replay or official preflight.

## Reading the status labels

- **Checked**: the corresponding mathematical conclusion has a Lean proof that has been compiled. A different proof is identified explicitly.
- **Checked, equivalent formulation**: the repository uses ordinary existence and universal upper-bound statements, or an equivalent integer inequality, instead of defining an extremal maximum or reproducing the paper's analytic parameter syntax.
- **In progress**: source has been written but the necessary final integration or compilation is still being completed. This must not be presented as checked.
- **Not separately formalized**: no standalone theorem for this auxiliary claim or original intermediate step was found. A consequence may follow from checked results, but this audit does not count an unwritten wrapper as a separately checked statement. This status must not be confused with an unproved principal result.
- **External/background**: a cited result, experimental observation, historical assertion, or physical implementation discussion is outside the mathematical proof developed in this repository.

All hypergraph conclusions below use `RigidGHZ.Hypergraph`, `PerfectMatching`, `RigidFamily` or `IsRigid`; finite certificates are connected to these ordinary definitions. A finite Boolean check alone is not counted as proving rigidity. Namespace prefixes are given in each table. Files live under `RigidGHZ/` unless otherwise stated.

The development currently does not define numeric functions literally named `f_r`, `g_r` or `ell_3`. Their lower bounds are represented by ordinary hypergraph witnesses; upper bounds quantify over every such witness. This is a standard equivalent presentation of the extremal assertions, but it should be stated when describing coverage.
