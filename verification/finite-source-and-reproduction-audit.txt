Finite theorem and certificate source audit

Scope: source-level statement comparison, deterministic generator consistency,
input coverage and SHA-256 verification. This supplements the successful Lean
builds; it does not claim an independent kernel rerun or a fresh solver run.

FiniteChallenge versus FiniteSolution
- six_vertex_exact: attainment of 10 and universal bound for every ordinary
  3-uniform H on Fin 6 and every natural d.
- tripartite_three_exact: attainment of 6 on Fin 9 with a balanced coloring
  into three parts of size 3; universal upper bound for every such H and d.
- tripartite_three_unique: canonical Fin 3 x Fin 3 hypergraphs with every edge
  transversal; the actual vertex equivalence preserves the first coordinate.
- nine_vertex_exact: attainment of 13 and universal upper bound for every
  ordinary 3-uniform H on Fin 9 and every natural d, without an upper cutoff.
  The proof for d >= 14 restricts the actual prescribed family to 14 matchings
  using RigidFamily.exists_restriction, then applies no_fourteen_on_nine.
- Hypergraph, Uniform, PerfectMatching, RigidFamily, IsRigid, BalancedPartite,
  tupleEdge, relabelEdge, relabelHypergraph, latinComplementGraph agree with
  their implementation definitions. PerfectMatching quantifies over actual
  vertices and actual edge membership; RigidFamily.complete quantifies over
  all ordinary perfect matchings.
- FiniteSolution does not import FiniteChallenge.
- NormalizedNinePair uses explicit parentheses around the intended iff inside
  the two non-equality hypotheses.

  and are covered by a matching SHA-256 entry in the two certificate manifests.
- Nine-vertex original CNF/metadata regeneration and dependency pruning from
  the distributed raw LRAT files reproduce every compared file byte-for-byte.
  The six pruned refutations contain 59,072 retained RUP steps.
- Supplementary table extraction from paper/supplement.tex reproduces the
  distributed 19-seed seeds.json byte-for-byte.
- The supplementary generator recreates 275 Lean data/facts/proof modules
  byte-for-byte from the retained, already checked core certificates.
- Regenerated supplementary original inputs and direct LRAT trace chunks
  match byte-for-byte. The 19 seeds use 126,630 retained RUP steps.
- Normalized affine cases are A29, A31, A41, A47. Default solve/prune/lean-proof
  skip those base cases; normalize handles their proved branches. No removed
  unnormalized candidate is required by the default commands.
- The external SAT solver was not rerun for this consistency check. Acceptance
  relies on the ordinary Lean proof terms reconstructed from the retained RUP
  traces, and the proved semantic bridge to actual perfect matchings.
